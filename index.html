Got Questions?

FAQ

How do I book an appointment? +

You can book directly through our website using the Book Now button, or call us at (413) 387-0110. We use Vagaro for all appointment scheduling.

What is your cancellation policy? +

We ask for at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations or no-shows may be subject to a fee. We appreciate your understanding — it helps us serve all our clients well.

How long does a color service take? +

Color services vary depending on the technique. A full balayage or highlight service typically takes 2.5–4 hours. We'll give you a more accurate time estimate during your consultation.

How long is an infrared sauna session? +

Sessions are typically 30–45 minutes. First-timers may want to start with a shorter session. All sauna sessions are private and sanitized between each use.

Where are you located and is there parking? +

We're located at 106 Russell St, Hadley, MA. There is free parking available on site.

Do you take walk-ins? +

We recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred time, but we welcome walk-ins based on availability. Give us a call at (413) 387-0110 to check same-day availability.

Why do you use organic products? +

We believe what goes on your hair and skin matters. Our products — including Innersense Organic Haircare, Cult+King, and Starling — are free of harsh chemicals, sulfates, and parabens. You get beautiful results without compromising your health.
